    🔴 Mandatory — Rome: Vatican & Colosseum

    🔴 Mandatory Rome — Vatican & Colosseum

    These are non-negotiable. Both require advance bookings — queue without tickets = 2–3 hours wasted.

    🔴 MANDATORY

    Colosseum + Roman Forum + Palatine Hill

    Plan: Wednesday Sep 16 morning

    The real gladiatorial arena where 50,000 people once watched combat. Add the Samsung Oculus VR — you stand in the arena while digital gladiators fight around you. Roman Forum included (the entire ancient city center).

    🎟️ Entry: €18/person (skip-the-line essential) 🥽 VR add-on: ~€30/person extra 📋 Book: coopculture.it — 2+ months ahead
    🔴 MANDATORY

    Vatican Museums + Sistine Chapel + St. Peter's

    Plan: Thursday Sep 17 morning (Wednesday is Papal Audience day — St. Peter's is restricted)

    Vatican Museums hold one of the world's greatest collections. The Sistine Chapel ceiling (Michelangelo) is genuinely breathtaking in person. St. Peter's Basilica is free — the Dome climb gives incredible Rome views.

    🎟️ Museums + Sistine: €22/person (€17 + €5 booking) ⛪ St. Peter's Basilica: FREE 🔭 Dome climb: €8 stairs / €10 lift 📋 Book: tickets.museivaticani.va — 2+ months ahead

    🌋 Pompeii Day Trip — ❌ DOES NOT FIT

    Kept here for reference only. Rome is 3 nights (Sep 15–18): Sep 15 is an evening arrival from Santorini, Sep 16 is the Colosseum, Sep 17 is the Vatican, and Sep 18 you fly to Geneva in the morning. Pompeii needs a full day (8am Frecciarossa Roma Termini → Naples 1.5h, then Circumvesuviana → Pompeii Scavi 40min, €18/person, back by 7pm) and there is no such day. Cutting either mandatory site to fit it would be the wrong trade — save Pompeii for a trip that starts in Naples.
